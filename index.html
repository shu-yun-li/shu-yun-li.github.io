My name is Shuyun Li. I am an HCI researcher and designer working across interaction design, garments, and textiles.

I am interested in how people experience technology beyond the screen, and how research and design can make everyday interactions more thoughtful, tangible, and human.

My background includes study at University of the Arts London and experience with Tencent Fintech, Tencent Games, and Carewear+.
